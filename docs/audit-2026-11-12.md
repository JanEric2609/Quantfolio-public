### 2.5 QuantML: is it machine learning, and does it work?

It is real ML, and it is correctly switched off.

- `lab/quant_lab/pooled_ml.py` trains ridge and LightGBM on 150 names, 10 price-only features, 20-day target ranked within each date, 5 purged walk-forward folds, Newey-West t with 20 lags. The design is sound.
- Latest run (11-07): LightGBM rank IC 0.025, t 1.77. Ridge IC 0.030, t 1.45. **12-1 momentum alone: IC 0.043, t 1.84.** The model adds nothing over one of its own inputs.
- Status every week: `rejected_below_gate`. So no ML output reaches a recommendation. The per-ticker models were last trained 09-26 and all lost to the majority-class baseline.

Why your feeling is right:

1. All ten features are transformations of the same price series. There is no new information for the model to find.
2. The gate cannot be passed with this sample. A t of 3 needs roughly ICIR × √(independent periods) ≥ 3. With 42 periods that is an ICIR of 0.46 per period; the measured one is about 0.2. Even with all bars back to 2019 (about 96 periods) it needs 0.31.
3. Bars exist for 1,455 symbols from 2019, but the study uses 150 names from late 2022.

Keep the gate. What would be a real test: a new pre-registered spec with non-price features (fundamentals, revisions, tone), the full universe and history, and the power stated up front.

### 2.8 Is the maths right and up to date?

Checked and correct (Sweep, with numeric cross-checks): Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, CVaR, beta/alpha/R², PSR, deflated Sharpe, PBO/CSCV, Newey-West t, Mincer-Zarnowitz, Ledoit-Wolf, band rebalancing, wealth projection, time-weighted return and XIRR, the tax rates in the trade check, look-ahead in backtests and labels.

Not correct:

1. **Diebold-Mariano promotion test** (finding 7). Newey-West with 20 lags on 40-170 overlapping dates understates the variance. Simulated rejection under the null: 2-5 % against a nominal 0.33 %. Fixes: use every 21st date, or calibrate the critical value by simulation.
2. **Capital ladder tests excess return, not alpha.** A sleeve with beta above 1 drifts up with no skill; Mandate B (mostly cash) drifts down in a rising market. Regress on the benchmark first.
3. **Ladder clipping at 3σ** is not mean-preserving. Fine for ordinary skew, broken for crash-prone return streams.
4. Efficient frontier substitutes an equal-weight point when the optimiser fails and reports it as a frontier point.

Display: confidence and FCF as above; several places bypass the central formatter (raw floats, en-US number format in a German UI, dot decimals).

More backtests would be the wrong use: every extra trial raises the bar the gates must clear. Uses that add information without adding trials:

1. **Null calibration.** Run the gates on shuffled labels and random signals to measure their real false-positive rate. This would have caught the Diebold-Mariano problem.

3. **Power tables**: for each gate, the smallest effect it can detect at the current sample.

6. **GPU**: `--parallel 1` and one job per night. A masked, frozen test of the tone signal on archived headlines is one GPU pass.

## 3. Big picture

The engineering is ahead of the evidence. Gates, ledgers, purged validation and sequential tests are implemented with care, and they keep reporting the same thing: no measurable stock-picking skill. On prod the resolved BUY picks are behind the index and conviction is inversely related to outcome. (The naive t of −5.9 overstates this because picks overlap; the sign and the conviction result do not depend on that.)

Meanwhile the part you actually read, reports, fundamentals and verdicts, has the weakest controls: free provider data is passed through with fixed labels, and a small model is asked to interpret it and to state confidence.

Both research sweeps point the same way:

- Active stock selection after costs and German tax on each realised gain is a hard game; the published factors that survive replication are the ones already in the composite, at IC levels where a 20-stock sleeve cannot harvest them.
- The best out-of-sample result found for LLM news tone is IC 0.020 (t 1.7), and the usable part is priced by the open.
- Valid sequential evidence is slow: in one published setup the median crossing took over 2,300 sessions at 15 bp a day.

So the ladder's 0 % is the correct state, not a malfunction. The useful work is in making what is shown true, and in learning from the machinery.

## 4. Suggested order of work

**A. Stop showing wrong things (small changes)**

1. Reject unknown tickers: an all-null provider answer is "not found", no score, no verdict.
2. Remove the verdict confidence number, and the horizons beyond one year.
3. FCF yield: require matching currencies, skip financials, flag companies with large minorities; otherwise show "n/a".
4. Net debt/EBITDA: label it "including leases".
5. 52-week range from unadjusted highs and lows.
6. Show `latest_period_end` beside growth figures and say "half-year" when the period is six months.
7. Report news: read the GDELT mentions and fetch company news on demand; say "no news source covers this company" when that is the case.

**B. Fix the statistics**

8. Diebold-Mariano: non-overlapping dates or a simulated critical value.
9. Ladder: beta-adjust before betting.
10. Drawdown starting value; frontier fallback.

**C. Data and pipeline**

11. Never store a bar dated on or after the exchange's current day; re-pull yesterday's bar after the close.
12. Misses: record only "provider answered, no data"; 20 h wait.
13. Take IC/ICIR out of the miner cache key, or run the precompute after 08:00.
14. News tone: `max_tokens`, JSON schema, temperature 0; count timeouts in the job status.
15. Numbers in reports from code, prose from the model, and reject output containing a number that is not in the fact table.

**D. Tests and CI**

16. Replace the `assert True` and exception-swallowing tests.
17. One test that warm keys equal read keys.
18. Report skips, add a coverage floor, add the downgrade check, take `docs/api/**` out of `paths-ignore`.

**E. Learning and research**

19. Learn tab as in 2.10.
20. Null-calibration and power tables on idle compute.
21. One pre-registered pooled study with non-price features on the full universe.
